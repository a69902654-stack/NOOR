🎓 NOOR — Light as a practice
A modern, full-page landing website for NOOR, a premium English language & study-abroad academy. Designed to guide ambitious students from foundational English to world-class universities through a structured, four-movement system.

📖 About the Project
NOOR is a single-page landing site built to present the academy's methodology, study tracks, pricing plans, and outcomes in a clean, editorial layout. The design uses a warm, premium aesthetic with dark/light section alternation, custom typography, and subtle micro-interactions.

The page is fully responsive and optimized for conversion — guiding visitors from the hero section all the way to the final call-to-action ("Reserve my seat").

✨ Sections Overview
#	Section	Purpose
1	Hero	Headline "Light the path from Konkur to the world" with stats (92% satisfaction, 1,240 students, 4.9 rating)
2	Universities	Logos of partner/target universities (Sharif, Tehran, Amirkabir, Toronto, UBC)
3	Inside the Package	Six core features: Konkur Engine, English to the world, Mentors, Adaptive practice, Study-abroad studio, Family progress portal
4	#FORCE — A studio, not a pile of PDFs	Dashboard preview + key metrics (400+ questions, 12,000 vocabulary, Weekly reports, Private)
5	Your Year Composed	Four phases: Foundation, Mastery, Pressure, Lift
6	Outcomes	What families actually see — rank movement, English fluency, plan B, mentorship
7	The Method — Four Movements	Map → Carve → Pressure → Rise
8	Voices	Testimonials from four students in four cities
9	Investment	Three pricing tiers: Roshd, Noor (featured), Kherad
10	FAQ	"Asked around the table" — accordion-style questions
11	Final CTA	"Eighty-six seats. Then we close the door." + Telegram link
12	Footer	Navigation, social links (Instagram, Telegram, LinkedIn), legal
🛠️ Tech Stack
HTML5 — semantic markup

CSS3 — custom properties, flexbox, grid, responsive design

JavaScript (Vanilla) — FAQ accordion, smooth scroll, mobile menu

Fonts — Custom serif + sans pairing (editorial feel)

Icons — Inline SVG

No frameworks, no build step — just clean, portable code.
